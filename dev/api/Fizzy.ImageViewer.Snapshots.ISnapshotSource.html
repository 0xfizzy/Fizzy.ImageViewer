Interface ISnapshotSource

Namespace
Fizzy.ImageViewer.Snapshots
Assembly
Fizzy.ImageViewer.dll

Captures independently owned snapshots of the committed frame or a region. At most two captures are admitted per Viewer, with one export read executing at a time. Further calls throw InvalidOperationException before acquiring a frame lease. Accepted calls capture the committed frame synchronously and may finish after Viewer closure.
